Plan your run

Search or tap to set a start and — if you like — a turn-around point the route passes through on the way back, plus optional stops to visit, and get up to 3 routes ranked by conditions right now — shade, heat, hills, traffic signals and how much is on paths & trails.

  • Choose a pin button, then tap the map to drop a pin at that exact spot.
  • A turn-around point is a turning point, not a destination: the route goes out to it and comes back, closing at your start.
  • "Loop back from a turn point" returns on different streets by default; "Out and back" retraces the same path.
  • Add up to 5 stops (a park, a water tap) — they're visited in the order you add them.
  • Slide the time forward to plan shade and UV for later in the day, then fine-tune it on the results screen.
  • Distance is optional — leave it blank for no target length.
